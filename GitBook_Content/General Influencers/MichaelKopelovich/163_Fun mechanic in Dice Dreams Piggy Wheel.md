Came across this fun mechanic in ‘Dice Dreams’ —Piggy Wheel! 🐷🎡 The event encourages players to “break the piggy” to earn a free spin on the wheel, adding an extra layer of excitement and reward to the gameplay. What’s even better is that some of the rewards tie directly into the pet event, which is a nice touch to keep players invested in multiple features at once.   
  
It’s a clever way to drive engagement while keeping things interactive and fun. 🎲💫
