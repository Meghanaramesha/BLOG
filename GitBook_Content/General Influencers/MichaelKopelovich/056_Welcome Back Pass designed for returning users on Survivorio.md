🔑 Spotted something new in [Survivor.io (Habby)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dxx.firenow&hl=en)— a Welcome Back Pass designed for returning users.

Most games lean on:  
🎁 Welcome back gifts (a simple bundle)  
🎯 Welcome back events (less common — e.g., MONOPOLY GO! has one)  
  
But this is the first time I’ve seen a special pass created just for reactivated players.  
  
Why it’s clever:  
✅ Adds structure & progression instead of a one-off reward  
✅ Encourages players to re-engage daily after coming back  
✅ Opens monetization opportunities (premium pass upsell)  
  
It’s a fresh twist on reactivation design — turning “welcome back” into an ongoing journey rather than a single moment.  
  
👉 Curious — do you think more games will shift from gifts → events & passes for returning users?
