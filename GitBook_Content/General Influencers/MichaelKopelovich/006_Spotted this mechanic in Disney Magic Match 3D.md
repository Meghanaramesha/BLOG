### [Spotted this mechanic in Disney Magic Match 3D!](https://www.gamigion.com/spotted-this-mechanic-in-disney-magic-match-3d/)

🎯 Spotted in Disney Magic Match 3D: a neat twist on level pressure and collectible rewards. Some levels now drop a Magic Item but only if you win on your first attempt. Miss it, and the item is gone until the next eligible drop a few levels later.Why I like it:✅ Turns regular level progression into a mini “high-stakes” moment✅ Adds light pressure without feeling punishing✅ Creates a natural rhythm: push harder every X levels to secure the reward✅ Keeps players engaged between events with something to look forward toSmall mechanic, big impact on session motivation.
