🚗 Just started playing Car Match by Grand Games and was pleasantly surprised by their Daily Task system.

Complete a few quick levels and unlock a Bonus Mission, a clever touch that sparked my curiosity. For new players, the first 8 levels are a breeze, so you get to the “secret” reward pretty fast. Simple, fun, and motivating.  
  
Nice example of using light mystery to drive early engagement.
