🎥 Talk about going the extra mile!  
While playing Last War, I noticed a really smart feature: after I failed a level, the game linked directly to an actual YouTube guide video showing how to beat that specific stage.

Such a simple but powerful UX touch — blending community-driven content and in-game help to keep players engaged instead of frustrated.
