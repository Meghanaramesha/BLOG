🎒 Back to School meets in-game offers

Following up on my recent post about Mob Control’s Tour De Mob (inspired by Tour de France) — I spotted another great example: a Back to School themed offer.  
  
Why it works:  
✅ It taps into a moment everyone recognizes  
✅ Keeps the game feeling fresh and relevant  
✅ Bundles the theme with extra value (x12 Skip’its here)  
  
I love how Mob Control consistently uses timely hooks — from sports events to seasonal shifts — to create offers that feel fun and contextual.  
  
👉 Do you prefer cultural tie-ins like sports, or seasonal ones like holidays and back-to-school campaigns?
