###### Interesting approach in Cake Sort by Lion Studios

Locking content behind in-game currency while also offering a direct purchase option for those who are short.

This adds a strategic choice: grind for coins or pay to skip. Especially smart since earning coins takes time, making the offer more tempting.   
  
Would you wait or buy?
