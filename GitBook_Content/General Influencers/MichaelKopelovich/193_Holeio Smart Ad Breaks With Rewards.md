**Ever wondered how to effectively integrate ads into short game sessions without frustrating your players?**

Hole.io nails it with their brilliant halftime ad break strategy in their 2-minute rounds. This approach not only aligns with Google policies but also enhances the player experience.

**Here’s how they do it:**  
⏲️ 5-second countdown alerts players before the break, making the transition smooth.  
  
🎁 Players see what they’ll earn after watching the ad, reducing the friction of ad-watching.  
  
🎥 Instead of a standard interstitial ad, players can opt to watch a reward video to gain an additional advantage (like starting the second half bigger for a better chance of winning).  
  
🚫 Finally, the option to remove ads altogether is offered, adding another monetization layer.  
  
This seamless integration keeps players engaged and turns potential frustrations into rewarding experiences. If you’re looking to refine your ad strategy, this is a method worth studying.
