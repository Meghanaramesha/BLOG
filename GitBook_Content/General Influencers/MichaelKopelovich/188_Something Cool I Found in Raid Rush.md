So, I’ve been playing this tower defense game [Raid Rush by Panteon](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wireless.defenseland&hl=en), and they did something really clever with their **Atlantis Rising** event that I wanted to share.

A few days before the event even starts, they’re slowly revealing content in timed slots, building up the hype and keeping me coming back to see what’s next!  
  
🕑 Each slot has a countdown, and every time one unlocks, it feels like opening a little present leading up to the main event. I love how they’re using this to build suspense—it’s a really smart way to keep players engaged before the event even goes live.  
  
If you’re looking for a fun example of how to keep players hooked in the days leading up to a big event, \*Raid Rush\* is nailing it with this teaser approach!
