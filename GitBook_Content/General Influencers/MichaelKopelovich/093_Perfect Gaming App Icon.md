📌 Shoutout to ‘Color Block Jam’ for this beautiful icon.   
It’s simple. It’s bold. It’s basically a LEGO brick — and they used the natural rounded-square icon shape to their full advantage. With a 3D feel, soft shadows, and no extra noise, this icon does exactly what it should: catch your eye and spark curiosity.  
  
Game icon design at its best.
