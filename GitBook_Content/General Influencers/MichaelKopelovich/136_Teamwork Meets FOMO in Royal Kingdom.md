###### 💡 Teamwork Meets FOMO in Royal Kingdom 💡

Royal Kingdom by Dream Games has added an interesting nudge to encourage Kingdom Pass purchases. When a teammate buys the pass, everyone on the team gets a small gift 🎁, and you also see who bought it and who the gift is from. 👀  
  
This creates a mix of [social proof] and [peer pressure]:   
👉 You see others buying the pass and feel the subtle urge to join in.   
👉 You know your purchase benefits the team, adding an altruistic spin.   
👉 It highlights who bought the pass, making it harder to resist and avoid feeling left out.   
  
It’s a clever mix of psychology and monetization—rewarding players and driving engagement at the same time. 🧠💰
