### [Royal Match really knows how to make small wins feel huge.](https://www.gamigion.com/royal-match-really-knows-how-to-make-small-wins-feel-huge/)

Royal Match really knows how to make small wins feel huge. Loved this moment where just before starting a new level, you're reminded you finished first in the event — and boom, that glowing reward chest drops in with a satisfying animation. Super clean design. Super motivating. One of the best at turning UI into a celebration.
