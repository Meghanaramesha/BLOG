🎮 Spotted a fun event in Minion Rumble! They built it around the viral fruit merge mechanic (drop → merge → level up) that exploded with Suika Game and similar titles.I really like how they turned a trending standalone genre into a limited-time event inside their game.✅ Fresh gameplay without leaving the ecosystem✅ FOMO + novelty factor to boost engagement✅ Smart way to ride the wave of a viral mechanicGreat example of how live-ops can take inspiration from popular genres and adapt them into events.👉 Curious — do you think we’ll see more games embedding these “mini viral genres” into events?
