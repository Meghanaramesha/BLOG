###### Twisted Tangle’s Smart Monetization Choices! 🔥

Just noticed a clever setup in “Twisted Tangle” by Rollic Games. When you run out of time in a level, they present three distinct offers:  
  
1️⃣ Season Pass – Great for long-term players.   
2️⃣ Booster Focused – For those looking for an edge in the level.   
3️⃣ Lives and Time Focused – Perfect if you’re aiming to keep the game going right away!  
  
This tailored approach gives players flexibility to choose what fits their play style best, with each option adding +20 seconds to keep you in the game. Cool, right? 🎯
