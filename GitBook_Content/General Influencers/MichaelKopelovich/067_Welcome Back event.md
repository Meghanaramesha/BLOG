🎉 We’ve all seen “Welcome Back” offers in mobile games…  
But a Welcome Back event?

MONOPOLY GO! takes it up a notch with “Welcome Treasures” — a special limited-time event that triggers when returning to the game after some time away.  
  
Instead of just offering a bundle, it gets you playing right away — a smart way to re-engage lapsed players through actual gameplay.  
  
Would love more examples!
