Spotted a clever twist in Chum Chum’s battle pass!

After you claim a reward from the free track, the game offers a rewarded video to grab that same reward again. 👀 It’s a smart way to add extra value to the free pass without affecting the premium tier — and a nice bonus for ad-friendly players.
