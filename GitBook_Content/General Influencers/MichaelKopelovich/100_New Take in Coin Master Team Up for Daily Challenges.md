###### Teaming Up for Daily Challenges – A New Take in Coin Master

Daily challenges have been a staple in mobile games, but Coin Master is testing an interesting twist. Instead of completing them solo, players can invite a friend or a team member to tackle the tasks together. This adds a social layer to progression, making the experience more engaging while increasing retention. Haven’t seen this approach tested before in daily missions – curious to see how players respond to it!
