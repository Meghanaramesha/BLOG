Players see their early progress as a row of checkmarks leading toward a chest, with upcoming unlocks teased along the way.  
  
Why I like this:  
✅ Makes progression tangible from the very start  
✅ Keeps new players motivated with clear short-term goals  
✅ Builds anticipation for rewards just a few levels ahead  
  
The design could be polished a bit more, but the idea is strong — turning the FTUE into a guided path with visible milestones.
