**Welcome back to another week of exciting mobile game discoveries!**

This time, we’ve got a thrilling mix of strategy, matching, and puzzle games that have kept me hooked all week. Let’s jump right in!

[](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/M-Video-1.mp4)

##### 1) TDS – Tower Destiny Survive by SayGames

**👑 King of the Castle (or Tower!)**  
In this super fun tower defense game, you’ll find yourself defending your very own tower against relentless waves of zombies. It’s not just about surviving; it’s about strategically building and fortifying your tower to withstand the onslaught. The cute animations combined with the challenging gameplay make this game a standout title. If you love strategy games that test your tactical thinking, this one’s for you!  
  
[App Store](https://shorturl.at/ZzzUI)[Google Play](https://shorturl.at/D9LSn)

##### 2) Triple Treats: Tile Match by Calumma Games

**🍰 A Sweet Debut!**  
Calumma Games has hit the ground running with its first release, Triple Treats. This adorable tile-matching game takes you on a sweet journey where your matching skills will help you create delectable bakery masterpieces. The beautiful visuals, paired with a fresh building meta system, make this game a delightful treat for both your brain and your eyes. Perfect for a relaxing yet engaging gaming session.  
  
[App Store](https://shorturl.at/WFMfX)[Google Play](https://shorturl.at/VVIju)

##### 3) Magic Sort by Blu Studios

**🔮 A Magical Twist on Sorting Puzzles**  
Magic Sort brings a new dimension to the classic water sort puzzle genre. Help Wizard George sort out his magical potions in this beautifully designed 3D puzzle game. The animations are incredibly smooth, making the gameplay both relaxing and challenging. If you enjoy puzzle games with a bit of magic, Magic Sort is worth a download.  
  
[App Store](https://lnkd.in/djVAbUVx)[Google Play](https://lnkd.in/ds5y3XDk)

###### That’s all for this week’s top game picks!

Each of these games offers a unique experience that’s sure to keep you entertained.

Give them a try, Happy gaming! 🎮
