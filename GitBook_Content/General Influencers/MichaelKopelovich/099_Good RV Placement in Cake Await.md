###### Good RV Placement in Cake Await

Really like this approach to rewarded video at the level start screen. Instead of a generic RV offer, the game presents three boosters—giving players a clear and immediate benefit for watching an ad. This placement makes the decision feel natural, reinforcing the value of RV while improving gameplay.   
  
A small but effective way to boost engagement and monetization without disrupting the player experience.
