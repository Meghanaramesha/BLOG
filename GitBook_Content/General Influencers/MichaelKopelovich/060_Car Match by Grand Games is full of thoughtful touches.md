🚗 Car Match by Grand Games is full of thoughtful touches, and this one caught my eye:

The Journey map not only visualizes your level progression, it cleverly drops rewards along the road (like 30m of infinite lives or bonus coins) to keep players motivated.  
  
Simple, clean, and satisfying.

Anyone else a sucker for a good milestone-based reward track? 🎯
