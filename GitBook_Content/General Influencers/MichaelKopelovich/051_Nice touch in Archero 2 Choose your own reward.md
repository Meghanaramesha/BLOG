🎁 Spotted a nice touch in Archero 2 — on the 7th day of the login calendar, players can choose their own reward.

#### Why I like this:

✅ Adds player agency to a standard retention mechanic  
✅ Makes the final day feel more exciting & personal  
✅ Reduces the “one size fits all” problem of static calendars  
  
Such a small change, but it transforms the 7-day login from routine into something players actually look forward to.  
  
👉 Do you think more games should let players customize rewards in daily calendars?
