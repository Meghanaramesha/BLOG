###### 🎮 Smart Design Meets Convenience! 🚀

I came across this intriguing feature in Color Clash by Voodoo. Typically, upgrading your troops requires winning battles to get upgrade packs. The catch? Only one pack can unlock at a time, so you have to keep coming back to start unlocking the next one.  
  
Here’s the twist: they’re now offering an \*auto-start\* option for a price! It’s a clever way to keep players engaged while introducing a monetization opportunity.
