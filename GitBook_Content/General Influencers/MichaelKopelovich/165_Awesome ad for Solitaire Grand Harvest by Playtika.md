Just saw this awesome ad for ‘Solitaire Grand Harvest’ by Playtika, showcasing their newest in-game collaboration with Snoopy! 🐶🌻 The mobile ad highlights Snoopy’s journey into the game world, complete with adorable challenges and the chance to build Snoopy’s camp. It’s all part of a six-month campaign celebrating Snoopy’s 75th anniversary. 🎉  
  
What really caught my eye is how they use the ad to feature new game events—a strategy I don’t see often in mobile gaming ads. It’s a clever way to engage users by giving them a glimpse of what’s new and exciting in the game. I think this approach could be great for attracting users and should definitely be A/B tested in marketing campaigns.
