🧩 Noticed this cross-promo in Word Trip by PlaySimple Games — a button on the main screen that opens up a portfolio of their other titles.

Cross-promotion is a powerful tool, especially when it’s timed around churn-prone moments (like D7–D10) to redirect users who might otherwise drop off.  
  
The only thing I’m unsure about here: the button seems to appear very early in the game. That might be too soon, potentially distracting for engaged players who are still in the core loop.
