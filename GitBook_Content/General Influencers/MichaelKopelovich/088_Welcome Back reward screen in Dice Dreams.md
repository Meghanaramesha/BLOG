###### Really liked this “Welcome Back” reward screen in Dice Dreams by SuperPlay.

Instead of dropping all the rewards at once, they use a timed collection system — claim one, start the countdown for the next. It’s a smart way to increase session time for returning players, nudging them to hop back into the gameplay and hopefully enjoy it again.
