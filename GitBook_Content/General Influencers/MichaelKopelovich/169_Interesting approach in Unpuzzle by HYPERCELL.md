I came across an interesting approach in ‘Unpuzzle’ by HYPERCELL. They’ve placed the Level Race event behind an RV (Rewarded Video) placement, meaning players need to watch an ad to unlock the event. 🏆🎥  
  
I’ve seen RV placements for rewards and boosts, but it’s the first time I’ve seen a game event itself gated this way. It’s a creative way to integrate ads while keeping players engaged and curious to participate. Really smart and definitely stands out!   
  
What do you think about gating events behind RVs—genius or too much?
