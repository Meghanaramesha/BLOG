📆 Here’s another example of an event gated behind a paywall — this time from Coin Master.

🎁The Rewards Road event locks progression until you make a one-time purchase, after which you can start collecting rewards along the path.  
  
✅It’s a similar strategy to what I saw recently in MONOPOLY GO!, and likely another A/B test to measure monetization impact vs. friction.
