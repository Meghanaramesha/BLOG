🎁 Missed a day in your daily login streak? No worries — watch an ad and recover your rewards!

Saw this clever implementation in This is Blast! by Voodoo. Instead of punishing players for missing a day or forcing them to start over, they offer a simple recovery option via rewarded video. 💡  
  
It’s a smart and fair mechanic that turns a missed session into a moment of re-engagement — and monetization.
