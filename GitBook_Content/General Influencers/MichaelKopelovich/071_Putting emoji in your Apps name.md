Wait… you can actually put emojis in your app’s name?

Just noticed the ⚔️ icon in Empires & Puzzles — and apparently it’s not some weird glitch. It’s part of the app name itself!  
  
Had no idea this was a thing, but turns out developers can use emoji or symbols in their App Store titles, and they’ll show up right on your home screen.  
  
Cool little trick for standing out — and now I’m curious how many other games do this…
