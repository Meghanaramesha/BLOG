🎯 Saw an interesting twist on daily goals in Cake Sort by Lion Studios!

Before starting the week, players get to choose their daily XP goal, and the rewards scale up based on how many days they stick with it.  
  
✅ Flexible goals  
📆 Progress resets weekly  
🎁 Bigger chests for longer streaks  
  
I’ve seen this goal-setting approach before in Coin Master and Travel Town — but usually in limited-time live-ops events, not in daily or weekly task systems.  
Nice mix of personalization and retention — and the UI keeps it light and motivating.
