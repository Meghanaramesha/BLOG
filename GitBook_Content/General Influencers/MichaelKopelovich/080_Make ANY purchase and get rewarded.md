🛒 Make ANY purchase and get rewarded?   
Saw this “Shopping Craze” screen in Fish of Fortune and loved how clear and generous it feels.

No tiers. No complexity. Just ‘buy anything’ and get bonus rewards.  
It’s a smart nudge — especially for hesitant spenders who might just need that extra push.  
  
Quick, colorful, and straight to the point.
