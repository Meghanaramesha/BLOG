🎯 Spotted an interesting Rewarded Video (RV) placement at the level start screen.

Instead of only showing boosters you already own, the game gives you the option to watch an ad to get an extra booster before you even begin.  
  
Why I like it:  
✅ Players make the decision at a high-motivation moment (right before tackling the challenge)  
✅ Reduces friction — no need to backtrack to the shop  
✅ Smart way to blend monetization with progression flow  
  
A small but clever placement that turns anticipation into opportunity.  
  
👉 Do you think RVs work better before a level starts, or after it ends?
