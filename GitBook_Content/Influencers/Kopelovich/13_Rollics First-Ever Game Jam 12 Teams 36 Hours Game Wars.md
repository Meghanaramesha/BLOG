[Rollic](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rollic) Istanbul office was very fun on Nov 29-30. What’s in?

*5,000+ applicants. 12 teams. 52 people. 36 hours. 1 Theme.*..

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-3-1240x790.jpeg)![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-9-1240x615.jpeg)

#### First, PRIZE?

Yep, best part 🤟🏻

*Not just careers or potential collabs.*

* MacBook Pro for every member of the winning team
* PlayStation 5 for all participants. COOL!

Beyond that, also great to experience this right at Rollic’s office.

Not just showing up to work there, but actually being together as teams, plus direct contact with Rollic Product & Publishing teams, even [CEO Burak Vardal](http://linkedin.com/in/burakvardal).

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-6-1240x733.jpeg)
![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-7-1240x665.jpeg)

One important detail worth mentioning.

Rollic received thousands of applications for this game jam.   
Though, just 12 Teams & 52 People.

That decision naturally sparked some debate. But the reasoning is pretty clear. Limited space, real teams, real prizes, real time with the people inside Rollic.

Honestly, this feels less like a one-off event and more like the beginning of a series.  
Wouldn’t be surprising to see Rollic run this kind of game jam regularly, maybe 3 or 4 times a year.

---

**Theme:** Holes  
**Challenge:** Build a fun, 3-level Puzzle Prototype  
**Tools:** Unity, Creativity & Very little sleep… 😄

*Inspired by Rollic*, every team explored the “*Hole*” mechanic in their own way.

Different mechanics, different feels, same time pressure.

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-4-1240x615.jpeg)

#### Format

* Solo or Team, all good.
* Delivering in 36 hours
* Submission includes:  
  “No builds needed, will be tested on Unity iOS Simulator.”

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-8-1240x772.jpeg)

#### Judging Criteria

* Gameplay Innovation
* Marketability & Hypercasual Readiness
* Visual Consistency & Clarity
* Prototype Completeness
* Fun Factor

#### Opportunities?

* Feedback from Rollic Product & Publishing Teams
* Exposure to Global Gaming Professionals
* Future Collab, Incubation, Publishing Deals, CAREERS. (Already there :))
* Awards & Recognition for High-potential Concepts

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-1-1240x668.jpeg)


---

🏆 **Out of 12, Team 5 took the win.**

A super smooth, creative & well-polished take on the theme that clearly stood out.

![](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Rollic-2-1240x790.jpeg)


---

Beyond the winner, the jam was about:

* Rapid prototyping
* Testing market-ready ideas fast
* Seeing how simple mechanics can evolve into fun core loops

*Big congrats to all teams shipping in 36 hours.*

To many more Rollic Game Jams ahead! 🤟🏻
