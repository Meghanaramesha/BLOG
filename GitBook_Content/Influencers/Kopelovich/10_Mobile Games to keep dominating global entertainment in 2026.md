Looking ahead to 2026, I’m convinced mobile games will continue to dominate global entertainment.

*What’s changing, though, is the mindset.*

> The industry is leaning toward more thoughtful, sustainable growth: better product quality, stronger retention, and a clearer focus on long-term player value. More studios are moving away from the old “scale at any cost” approach and putting their energy into growth that they can actually measure and maintain.

This shift is already shaping how teams prepare for next year and probably the years after.

---

*Store diversification is also becoming a major strategic priority.*

With more third-party and regional stores gaining traction, developers suddenly have far more options for distribution. Yes, it adds complexity, but it also opens up new audiences, new promotional routes, and more control over where and how games are presented. This trend will heavily influence how studios think about scaling in 2026.

And as part of that, non-traditional user acquisition will play a much bigger role.

---

Creator-led funnels, brand collaborations & experiential marketing are all set to grow in importance.

*These approaches don’t just boost performance.*

They help build community and create a stronger emotional bond with players. With traditional performance channels becoming harder to predict, the studios that manage to blend creativity with solid data will be the ones that pull ahead.
