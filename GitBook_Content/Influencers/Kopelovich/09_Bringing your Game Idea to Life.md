###### 

AI has made it possible for anyone to build a game.

Over the past few months we’ve spoken to established studios, VC-backed founders, and people completely outside gaming, all using AI to build and test ideas.  
  
*The wave of creativity this unlocks is incredibly exciting.*  
Moving from an idea to a game you can launch and scale is still a tough challenge though, particularly for anyone outside the industry.  
  
If that’s you, here are a few questions worth thinking through early:  
  
**What games do you play?**

* What do you like about them?
* What would you change?

**Who is your game for?**

* What art style might appeal to that type of player?
* What gameplay would they enjoy?
* What would they want to achieve in the game?

**How will it make money?**  
– Some players who enjoy your game will want to spend money. Giving them something valuable to buy is a win-win.  
– Or ads, which can be tuned to balance income with a good player experience.  
  
And finally, what’s the goal of the project?  
  
– Side hustle or world domination?  
– Both are fine, but lead to very different decisions.  
  
The mistake we see most often is starting to build before you understand what you’re trying to achieve, or whether players are interested.  
  
Putting your idea in front of people who’ve shipped games before is the best first step. They can help smooth off the edges and define a tight first vertical-slice you can actually put in front of players.  
  
This is work we’ve done many times, with established teams and first-time builders. If you’re thinking about turning a game idea into something real, feel free to get in touch.
