###### What is the Grid Scavenger Hunt and Why is it Effective?

[](https://www.gamigion.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/video.mp4)

**Event Mechanics:**  
  
✅ Players earn scissors by completing orders, which are used to remove tiles in a grid.  
  
✅ Some tiles contain event items needed for progression.  
  
✅ Rewards like energy and chests are given for collecting all items. A progress bar tracks additional rewards.  
  
✅ Players can obtain extra scissors through special event monetization offers or by using hard currency, adding a sink for in-game resources.  
  
**Impact and Versatility:**  
  
✅ This event stabilizes midweek revenue drops rather than causing spikes.  
  
✅ The Grid Scavenger Hunt is a flexible tool for patching event schedules and providing fresh experiences.  
  
✅ From my experience, it is highly rewarding at the start and purchase-provoking towards the end, making it effective throughout the week.  
  
The strategic timing and rewarding nature of this event make it effective for maintaining player engagement and stabilizing revenue.
