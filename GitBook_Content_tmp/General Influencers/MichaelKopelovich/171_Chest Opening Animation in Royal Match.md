###### I’ve been playing Royal Match, and I have to say, the chest opening animation and rewards are absolutely fantastic! The chest jumping, the smooth animation, and the way the rewards are presented (plus the amount!) make it such a satisfying experience. It’s just the right balance—nothing over the top, but enough to feel really rewarding. 👏

Dream Games really nailed it with this feature. It’s those small touches that make a huge difference! 🎉
