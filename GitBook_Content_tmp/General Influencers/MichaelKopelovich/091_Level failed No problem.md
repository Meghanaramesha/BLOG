###### Level failed? No problem — here comes the \*nudge\*.

While playing Match Factory by Peak, I spotted this smart little UX touch: after failing a level, the game gives you the option to activate boosters for 1 hour and jump right back in.  
  
It’s a simple yet effective tactic — no hard sell, just a well-timed prompt to keep players engaged and progressing.   
Small design choices like this can have a big impact on session time and retention.  
  
Loved seeing this one in action 👏
