Really liked this small UI touch in Block Jam 👇

When you’re down to just 1 space left on the board, the game highlights it in red AND adds arrows pointing to your available boosters — a clever way to nudge players toward using them.  
  
It might show up a bit too often, but still an interesting example of soft pressure through visual cues.
