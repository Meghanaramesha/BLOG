###### 🎯 Reaching Every Player Type!

Loving this smart design from Collect Em All! by Voodoo during their live-ops event. Players get multiple ways to keep participating: use in-game currency, watch rewarded ads, or grab a special in-app purchase offer. It’s a great way to cater to both free-to-play players and paying users alike.   
  
This approach ensures accessibility while maximizing engagement and monetization opportunities.
