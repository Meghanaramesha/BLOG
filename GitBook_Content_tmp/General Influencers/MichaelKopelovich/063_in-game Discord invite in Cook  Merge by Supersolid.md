🎁 Love seeing this kind of in-game Discord invite in Cook & Merge by Supersolid!

A great reminder that community-building doesn’t have to live only on social media or the app store.  
Bringing players into a space where they can trade, chat, and engage outside the game can deepen retention, especially for collection-based mechanics like card trading.   
  
On a personal note, I truly believe we’ll see a growing demand for professionals who specialize in community-building.  
Not just social media managers, but true community architects with expertise in fostering engagement, trust, and belonging.
