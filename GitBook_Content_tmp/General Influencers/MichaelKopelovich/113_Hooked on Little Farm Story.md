###### 🌾 Hooked on Little Farm Story! 🚜

Discovered a new idle game I’m obsessed with—Little Farm Story by SayGames & H8Games, a studio from Ukraine! 🇺🇦   
  
Loving the tools screen—it blends in-game currency, rewarded video, and IAP in a clever way. You can unlock tools individually or get everything for 7 days with a one-time purchase. Smart design that caters to different player types! 🎯
