##### Here’s a great example of an effective phone connect funnel that I saw in Coin Master by Moon Active.

**This simple yet engaging process makes it easy for players to connect their phone numbers and rewards them along the way.**

It’s a strategy that other game developers can learn from to enhance player engagement and retention.  
  
**How It Works:**  
✅ Step 1: Enter Your Phone Number  
  
✅ Step 2: Enter the Verification Code  
  
✅ Step 3: Receive Your Rewards  
  
✅ Step 4: Add Friends and Get Additional Rewards  
  
This method is straightforward and keeps the user engaged throughout the process by offering immediate rewards.

It’s an excellent way to integrate players more deeply into the game ecosystem and ensure they feel valued.
