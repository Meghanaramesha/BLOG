###### 🎡 Wheel of Cats… with a twist!

While playing ‘Find The Cat’ by Agave Games, I came across something that genuinely surprised me in the best way: a free spin wheel where one of the rewards is… a 3-day free trial to remove ads 😮  
  
Why I like it:  
  
✅ Encourages players to spin   
✅ Introduces the subscription passively   
✅ Makes winning feel exciting and generous  
  
Smart, playful, and effective. More of this please.
